L1 Short writing

Paper 2 style shorter writing

2 marks

Describe the location of Nigeria. Use compass directions, the coast and neighbouring countries.

Possible answer: Nigeria is in West Africa on the Gulf of Guinea / Atlantic coast, close to the equator. It borders Benin to the west and Cameroon to the east, with Niger and Chad to the north.
Self-mark: 1 mark for broad location; 1 mark for accurate extra detail such as coast or neighbouring country.

4 marks

Explain why Nigeria is important at a global scale. Build two developed reasons with evidence.

Possible answer: Nigeria is globally important because it produces about 8% of the world’s oil and is a member of OPEC, so it influences energy markets. It is also Africa’s largest economy and has a huge population, making it an important market and regional power.
Self-mark: Award up to 2 marks per developed reason. Credit accurate evidence.

3 marks

Describe the employment structure of Nigeria. Use the sector percentages in your answer.

Possible answer: About 35.9% work in the primary sector, around 14.3% in secondary and roughly 49% in tertiary. This shows services are now the biggest employer, while manufacturing remains smaller.
Self-mark: Credit accurate sector percentages and a clear comparative statement.

L1 Exam practice

Development within a country

4 marks

Explain why core and periphery regions exist in Nigeria. Build two reasons and develop each.

Investment has been concentrated in the South, especially Lagos and the Niger Delta, because oil reserves and ports attract business. Colonial rule also focused development on the coast, leaving many northern areas with weaker infrastructure and fewer jobs.

3 marks

Explain one way investment by transnational corporations has increased the development of Nigeria. Use a named example if you can.

TNC investment can create jobs and bring infrastructure. In Nigeria, oil companies have helped generate exports and government revenue, which can be used on roads and services.

8 marks

Assess the importance of the different reasons that have led to the growth of a core region and less developed regions on the periphery.

Possible reasons: oil in the South; port access; colonial investment; Lagos as a growth pole; Abuja as a second core. Judgement: economic factors matter most, but they were strengthened by history and infrastructure.

4 marks

Explain how trade helps Nigeria to develop.

Trade brings in foreign currency and export earnings. Oil exports are worth a lot, so the government gains revenue it can spend on development. Trade can also create jobs in transport, ports and processing.

4 marks

Explain why social inequalities exist between urban and rural areas in Nigeria.

Urban areas such as Lagos have better funded hospitals, schools and infrastructure. Rural areas often have weaker services, so fewer children attend school and poverty is harder to escape.

L2 Short writing

Paper 2 style questions

2 marks

Explain one way a geopolitical relationship has affected the development of Nigeria.

Nigeria’s role in ECOWAS and peacekeeping can improve regional stability, which helps trade and investment.

4 marks

Explain how technology has helped Nigeria to develop. Use examples.

Technology helps development because mobile banking platforms such as Paga make business transactions easier and faster. E-learning and telemedicine also connect people in remote areas to services they might otherwise miss.

3 marks

Explain one way a lack of food security can limit a country’s rate of development. Apply the idea to Nigeria.

A lack of food security can cause malnutrition and ill health, so people are less productive at work or in school. This slows economic development.

Describe the negative environmental impacts of rapid development in Nigeria.

Oil spills damage rivers and farmland, forest loss reduces biodiversity, and Lagos produces huge amounts of waste that can pollute land and water.

Final exam practice

Plan and write

3 marks

Explain one way investment by transnational corporations has increased development in Nigeria. Use Shell or another example.

Use: jobs, tax revenue, infrastructure, technology transfer, multiplier effect. Stronger answers include a named example and a clear link to development.

4 marks

Explain two ways transnational corporations can raise the level of development in a country.

Two developed points from: jobs, tax revenue, exports, infrastructure, skills and technology transfer. You can include a counterpoint that profits may leave the country.

8 marks

Evaluate whether the positive impacts of rapid development outweigh the negative impacts in Nigeria.

Social: some better schooling/services but Makoko and inequality remain. Economic: GDP growth and investment. Environmental: oil spills, deforestation and waste. Judgement should weigh economic growth against social/environmental costs.

8 marks

Evaluate the success of top-down and bottom-up development strategies in Nigeria.

Top-down projects can work at larger scale but are expensive and less community-led. Bottom-up projects are more local and targeted but harder to scale up. Strong judgement compares scale, sustainability and who benefits.
